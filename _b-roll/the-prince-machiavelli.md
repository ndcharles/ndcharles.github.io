---
title: "What Machiavelli gets right about running operations"
date: 2026-09-25 19:30:00 +0100
tags: [leadership, books]
---
People quote *The Prince* as a manual for scheming. Read it again and a lot of it is about something duller: keeping a system stable after you inherit it.

Machiavelli's point is that a new ruler is judged on what happens, not on what was intended. Operations works the same way. Nobody remembers the elegant plan for the migration; they remember whether payroll ran on Friday.

The part I keep coming back to: change the rules in one go, then stop. Drip-feeding painful changes keeps everyone anxious for months. Make the hard call once, explain it, and let people settle.
