Sample note. Most internal tools I have shipped started life as a Google Sheet that one team refused to stop using. That is not a failure of the tool. It is the requirements document, already tested by real users for months. Before you replace the sheet, sit with the people who use it and watch which columns they touch, which ones they ignore, and which formulas they are scared to delete. That is your spec. Everything else is decoration.
