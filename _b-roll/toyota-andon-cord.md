---
title: "Toyota lets anyone stop the line. Do your dashboards?"
date: 2026-09-25 22:45:00 +0100
tags: [operations, data, lean]
---
On a Toyota assembly line, any worker who spots a defect can pull the andon cord and stop production. Stopping is expensive, and Toyota does it anyway, because a defect caught at the station costs far less than one found by a customer.

Most data teams have the opposite culture. A number looks off, someone notices, and the report goes out anyway because the meeting is at 10.

Give people a cord: a clear way to flag "this looks wrong" and hold a report without asking permission. Then ask the five whys when they pull it.
