title: "The one law from The 48 Laws of Power I actually use"
date: 2026-09-25 21:10:00 +0100

Most of *The 48 Laws of Power* reads like a guide to being exhausting at work. I don't recommend running a team on it.

But "Never outshine the master" has a useful, less cynical reading for anyone in data. When your analysis makes a manager's decision look wrong, the numbers don't land on their own. Bring them the finding first, in private, and let them carry it into the room. The insight still ships, and you keep the ally you need for the next one.
